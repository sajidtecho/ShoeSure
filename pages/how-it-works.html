How Selling & Service Requests Work on ShoeSure

A simple step-by-step guide for cobblers, shoe sellers, and customers using the ShoeSure platform.

Simple 4-Step Process

1

Sign Up For Free

Register as a customer, cobbler, or seller with basic details and phone verification.

2

List or Request

Sellers list footwear catalogs; customers submit renovation/customization requests.

3

Connect & Fulfill

Chat directly with cobblers, confirm shoe specifications, and hand off for delivery or repair.

4

Fast Delivery & Payout

Receive doorstep shoe delivery; sellers & artisans get direct bank payouts.

The ShoeSure Panel is your all-in-one control center. Manage customer orders, track shoe repair job statuses, view real-time earnings, and chat directly with buyers.
