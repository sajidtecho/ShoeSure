Common Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about doorstep repairs, pricing, and seller onboarding.

How does doorstep shoe repair pickup work?
When you place a repair order, a logistics courier agent collects your shoes from your doorstep. They are safely transported to certified ShoeSure cobbler workshops, repaired, and delivered back to your home address.
Is the commission really 0% for sellers and cobblers?
Yes! ShoeSure charges 0% commission fees on all products and artisan service sales. Sellers and cobblers keep 100% of their earnings with fast direct bank settlements.
What materials are used for custom sneaker artwork?
Artisans use professional waterproof acrylic paint designed specifically for leather and canvas footwear, sealed with a scratch-resistant scratch guard topcoat.
How long does a shoe restoration or repair take?
Standard cleaning and minor repair jobs take 2 to 3 days from pickup to delivery. Complete leather sole replacement and custom artwork take approximately 4 to 5 days.
